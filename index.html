Formula Sheet (Chapters 1 to 8)

Ch 1: Real Numbers

  • HCF × LCM = a × b (for two positive integers a and b).
  • Fundamental Theorem: Every composite number can be uniquely factorized into prime numbers.

Ch 2: Polynomials

  • Quadratic Polynomial: ax² + bx + c
  • Sum of zeroes (α + β) = -ba
  • Product of zeroes (αβ) = ca

Ch 3: Pair of Linear Equations

  • Unique Solution: a₁a₂ ≠ b₁b₂
  • Infinitely Many Solutions: a₁a₂ = b₁b₂ = c₁c₂
  • No Solution: a₁a₂ = b₁b₂ ≠ c₁c₂

Ch 4: Quadratic Equations

  • Standard Form: ax² + bx + c = 0
  • Discriminant (D): b² - 4ac
  • Quadratic Formula: x = -b ± √(b² - 4ac)2a
  • If D > 0: Two distinct real roots | If D = 0: Two equal real roots | If D < 0: No real roots.

Ch 5: Arithmetic Progressions (AP)

  • n-th term (an): a + (n - 1)d
  • Sum of n terms (Sn): n2 [2a + (n - 1)d] or n2 (a + l)

Ch 6: Triangles

  • Thales / BPT Theorem: If a line is drawn parallel to one side of a triangle intersecting the other two sides, it divides them in the same ratio.
  • Similarity Criteria: AAA, SAS, SSS.

Ch 7: Coordinate Geometry

  • Distance Formula: √[(x₂ - x₁)² + (y₂ - y₁)²]
  • Section Formula: (m₁x₂ + m₂x₁m₁ + m₂ , m₁y₂ + m₂y₁m₁ + m₂)
  • Midpoint Formula: (x₁ + x₂2 , y₁ + y₂2)

Ch 8: Trigonometry

  • sin θ = PH , cos θ = BH , tan θ = PB
  • tan θ = sin θcos θ , cot θ = cos θsin θ
  • sec θ = 1cos θ , cosec θ = 1sin θ
  • Identities: sin²θ + cos²θ = 1 | 1 + tan²θ = sec²θ | 1 + cot²θ = cosec²θ
